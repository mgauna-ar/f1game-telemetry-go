## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Core Telemetry Engine:** High-performance Go backend with 10Hz consolidated streaming, SQLite persistence, and strict 1:1 binary UDP decoding for F1 2025 and F1 2026 DLC specifications.
- **Live Pit Wall & Race Control:** Real-time timing leaderboard tower, Active Aero and Boost status indicators, dynamic weather radar with compound crossover recommendations, and incident ticker.
- **AI Race Engineer & Cockpit Radio:** Two-way voice radio with global steering wheel and keyboard Push-to-Talk (PTT), neural TTS voices, realistic cockpit audio simulation, and proactive pit wall telemetry monitoring across 9 vehicle subsystems.
- **Lap Comparator & Track Map:** Distance-normalized side-by-side telemetry traces (Speed, Throttle, Brake, Gears, ERS, and 2026 Active Aero), apex speed deltas, and synchronized interactive track visualizer.
- **Session History & Analytics:** Comprehensive session browser with league multi-tagging, batch ZIP export/import, dynamic race progression charts, tyre stint degradation timelines, and sector theoretical lap rankings.
